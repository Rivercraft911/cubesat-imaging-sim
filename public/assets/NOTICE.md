# Imagery credits

`earth-day.jpg` and `earth-night.jpg`: Solar System Scope / INOVE, [Earth textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Unmodified publisher JPEGs. The full license is included as `CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

`earth-clouds.webp`: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Reto Stöckli; compositing enhancements by Robert Simmon. [Blue Marble cloud composite](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/), [NASA imagery terms](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). The 2001 multiday source receives a documented display transfer and area reduction to 8192 × 4096, then lossless WebP encoding. Static appearance, not simulated-date weather. No NASA endorsement is implied. Exact sources, transformations and hashes for these three assets are in `earth-provenance.json`; copies were retained from the local Yapling observatory assets.

`bay-preview.webp`: USDA / USGS [National Map imagery](https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSImageryOnly/MapServer). The opening mosaic joins zoom-12 cached tiles without geographic resampling. It encodes an intermediate JPEG quality 94, masks near-black missing coverage (all RGB channels ≤2), and encodes WebP quality 94. `bay-preview.json` records bounds, tile addresses and hash; `scripts/cache-bay.py` reproduces it with Pillow. Aerial acquisition dates vary and are not the simulated pass date.

Bundled telephoto patches and runtime shot detail use [USDA NAIP 2022 via CDFW](https://gis.wildlife.ca.gov/images/rest/services/Base_Remote_Sensing/NAIP_2022/ImageServer). See [patch provenance](shots/NOTICE.md). Runtime exports use bilinear EPSG:3857 rasters with source alpha and a bounded local browser cache. Reported raster spacing describes the fetched grid, not a measured optical resolution or guarantee of source detail.

`earth-western-us.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon, [Blue Marble Next Generation](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/), August 2004 composite. Native 4096 × 4096 crop of the published 500 m product class, with no upsampling or invented detail. Exact source, bounds, processing and hashes are in `earth-western-us.json`. NASA imagery terms apply; no endorsement is implied. It supplies regional Earth detail below aerial coverage.

`earth-night-western-us.webp`: NASA Earth Observatory, [Black Marble 2016](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights). Native 500 m annual-composite crop, encoded as WebP; bounds, source and hashes are in `earth-night-western-us.json`. It is a static night-light reference, not simulated-date illumination. Runtime aerial coverage masks taper inward at missing-data edges; valid RGB remains unchanged and the satellite layer supplies gaps.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025. The `bay-regional.webp` mosaic uses six Sentinel-2B Collection 1 Level-2A true-color tiles from the same March 24, 2025 pass. `scripts/cache-sentinel.py` mosaics their native 10 m UTM grids and area-averages the result onto a Web Mercator grid with about 18.9 m local ground spacing. `bay-regional.json` records source products, exact grid bounds, coverage checks and the output checksum. [Earth Search source collection](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/collections/sentinel-2-c1-l2a) · [Copernicus Sentinel data legal notice](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice)

The wider `bay-regional-wide.webp` uses nine tiles from the same Sentinel-2B pass. `scripts/cache-sentinel.py --wide` mosaics their 20 m COG overviews and area-averages the result onto a 7347 × 8192 Web Mercator grid with about 27.3 m ground spacing at the center. The crop covers the square 25 mm Stanford view near closest approach. `bay-regional-wide.json` records the tile identifiers and verified coverage.

`earth-clouds-western-us.png`: native 2048 × 2048 crop of the same NASA 2001 cloud composite, in the 1 km product class. Exact crop, bounds, source and hash are in `earth-clouds-western-us.json`. The runtime applies the same documented display transfer as the global cloud map; cloud height and lighting remain illustrative.

`west-coast-regional.webp` uses March 25, 2025 UTC Terra MODIS Corrected Reflectance (True Color) from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). `scripts/cache-west-coast.py` assembles four geographic WMS exports of the nominal 250 m browse product and encodes the 8192 × 8192 texture as WebP. The observed scene includes coastal water, marine clouds and a swath join near the far northwest edge. Exact bounds, sample spacing, request URLs and checksums are in `west-coast-regional.json`. [NASA GIBS attribution guidance](https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/#data-use-guidance-and-acknowledgements)
